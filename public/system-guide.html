First-Day Onboarding

Simple 4-Step Daily Employee Routine

Follow this flow every workday for accurate attendance and productivity records.

1

GPS Punch In

Upon arriving within 150m of office premises, click "Punch In". Location coordinates and shift countdown begin.

2

Review Tasks

Open the "Task Tracker" to view single or shared team tasks assigned to you. Update progress percentages as you work.

3

Log Work Done

Before leaving, enter completed project deliverables, estimated vs actual hours, and notes in the "Work Done" tab.

4

Punch Out at EOD

Click "Punch Out" when wrapping up your workday. Net hours deducting Lunch & Tea breaks are computed automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for team members
What if my device GPS drifts or is slightly off?

The geofence is calibrated with a generous 150-meter perimeter. Briefly toggling your Wi-Fi on calibrates high-accuracy mobile GPS instantly.

Can multiple staff members update a team task?

Yes! Any staff member assigned to a shared team task can log progress, and completing the task logs verified work done for all assignees.

How do I get mobile notifications?

Click the Bell icon in the top header and select "Enable Push Notifications". You will receive native device alerts whenever someone mentions you or decisions your leaves.
