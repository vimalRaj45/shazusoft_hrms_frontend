🚀 First-Day Employee Quick Start

Follow these 4 simple steps every workday for effortless compliance and visibility:

1

Punch In via GPS

Upon arriving at office (within 150m of designated premises), click "Punch In". Your coordinates and login timestamp are recorded securely.

2

Track Tasks & Collaboration

Check the "Task Tracker" board for tasks assigned to you (individual or multi-staff team tasks). Update status as you progress.

3

Log Daily Work Done

Before leaving, enter tasks completed, project names, and hours spent in the "Work Done" tab for management review.

4

Punch Out at EOD

Click "Punch Out" on the dashboard. Net working hours (deducting recorded break times) are instantly calculated and stored.

📦 Complete Module Walkthrough

Everything you need to know about the software tools and workflows:

📍

GPS Geofence Attendance

150m Office Perimeter

Ensures honest, tamper-proof attendance without physical biometric hardware. Uses high-accuracy device GPS.

  • Dynamic Location Verification: System checks your device GPS against office latitude/longitude (150-meter radius).
  • Punch In / Punch Out: Single-click punch with real-time shift countdown and overtime indicators.
  • Break Tracking: Dedicated Lunch and Tea break timers to calculate accurate net office working hours.
  • Office Grace Period: Standard 10:00 AM shift time with automatic late marking past grace limits.
Pro Tip: Enable location permissions in your browser when prompted to allow automatic geofence checks.
⏱️

Timesheet & Regularization

Past-Days Review

Review historical working hours, attendance consistency, and request corrections for missed punches.

  • Past-Days Timesheet: Review daily login, logout, break duration, and net hours (prevents confusing future rows).
  • One-Click Regularization: If you forgot to punch or faced GPS glitches, submit a regularization request with reason.
  • Manager Approval Workflow: Admins can review, approve, or reject regularization requests with remarks.
  • Search & Filter: Filter timesheet records by date range, month, or status.
Pro Tip: Regularization requests update timesheet hours automatically upon manager approval.
👥

Multi-Staff Task Tracker

Team Collaboration

Managers can assign critical project tasks to multiple team members simultaneously with shared accountability.

  • Multi-Staff Selection: Assign 1 task to 1, 3, or all active staff members with individual checkboxes and quick "Select All" button.
  • Priority & Deadlines: Categorize tasks into Urgent (P0), High (P1), Medium (P2), or Low (P3) with strict due dates.
  • Progress Reporting: Any assigned staff member can report progress (0-100%) and work notes.
  • Auto WorkDone Logging: Marking a task completed automatically logs verified completion entries for each assigned staff member.
Pro Tip: Filter tasks by "My Assigned Tasks" or search by project title in the Task Tracker.
✍️

Daily Work Done Logger

Accountability

Maintain transparency and track project progress by recording daily activities and deliverables.

  • Daily Activity Logging: Record project names, task objectives, estimated hours, and actual hours spent.
  • Search & Filter: Search past work logs by date, project, or keyword.
  • Manager Review: Management can inspect team productivity logs in real-time.
  • Export Capability: Download your work log history for monthly reporting.
Pro Tip: Enter your work log daily before 6:30 PM to ensure accurate monthly productivity calculations.
🏖️

Leaves Quota & Permissions

Live Quota Cards

Transparent policy quotas for Casual, Sick, and Paid leaves alongside 2-hour monthly permission passes.

  • Real-Time Balance Cards: Instant visibility into remaining Casual (1/mo), Sick (1/mo), and Paid leave allowances.
  • Short Permission Passes: Request emergency short permissions (up to 2 hours per pass, max 2 passes per month).
  • Single & Multi-Day Requests: Simple date picker with automated day count calculations.
  • Instant Notifications: Receive push notifications when management reviews and decisions your application.
Pro Tip: Always apply for planned leaves at least 2 days in advance for smooth manager approval.

Self-Evaluations & Appraisal

Monthly Review

Structured monthly self-evaluations promoting personal growth, constructive feedback, and fair appraisals.

  • KPI Self-Scoring: Rate yourself across key competencies including Code Quality, Ownership, Timeliness, and Teamwork.
  • Accomplishments & Challenges: Document key achievements and roadblocks faced during the month.
  • Manager Feedback: View manager feedback, counter-ratings, and constructive appraisal notes.
  • Digital Signature: Sign off digitally with a formal declaration for HR records.
Pro Tip: Be thorough and specific in your monthly work summary to highlight your contributions during appraisal cycles.

Issue Resolution & Chat Hub

@Mention Enabled

Collaborative support ticket system for IT assistance, payroll questions, policy clarifications, and task inquiries.

  • Live Conversation Threads: Message back and forth with management in real-time on specific tickets.
  • @Mention Autocomplete: Type @Name to tag any active team member; they receive immediate push notifications and full ticket access!
  • Scope Filtering: Toggle between "All Issues", "Raised by Me", and "Mentioned In" with dedicated badges.
  • Resolution Tracking: Track ticket status through Open, In-Progress, Resolved, and Closed with audit history.
Pro Tip: When raising an issue concerning a teammate, tag them with @Name so they can participate in the thread.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What if I punch in outside the 150m geofence?

The system will display a distance warning with your current coordinates. If you are on an off-site client visit, submit a regularization request noting your client location.

Can multiple staff complete a shared task?

Yes! Collaborative tasks show all assignees. Any assigned member can update progress or complete the task, logging verified hours for everyone involved.

How do I receive instant push alerts?

Click the bell icon in the top header and click "Enable Push Notifications". When an admin updates your leave or mentions you, you'll receive a native device alert.
